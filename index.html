★ THE EXERCISE ★
1 ► CONVERSATION

Talk the hand through before you write anything down. What would you actually see, hear and feel in that room? Disagreement about that is the exercise.

2 ► JOURNEY

Keep the first two cards and draw a new impact three times. Same moment, three places to look. Then keep the group card and re-draw the move instead.

3 ► REFLECTION

Ask it twice: how does this land in the room, and how does it read two weeks later? A move that scores well now and thin later is worth a second look.

◆ HEAD

Cognitive. Thoughts, beliefs, the rule they infer about what is allowed here. Limiting or enabling.

▲ HEART

Affective. Feelings, and their price. Confidence, calm and readiness give energy back; worry, fear and watchfulness cost more than they give.

● HANDS

Behavioural. What they do next and what they stop doing. Speak or wait, look away, avoid the group, avoid naming the thing.

The Facilitation Gym — eleven facilitation muscles, in development. The group and facilitator decks are drawn from the Gym's scenario cards; the impact deck crosses two subjects, the group and you, with three channels.
