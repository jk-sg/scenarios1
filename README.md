# Scenario Designer

A scenario generator for **The Facilitation Gym** — eleven facilitation muscles, in development.

Draw three cards and read the hand as one sentence:

> When **[the group]**, and you **[the facilitator]**, trace the impact on **[the impact]**.

Draw each card on its own or all three at once. 47 group behaviours × 64 facilitator moves ×
30 impacts = 90,240 hands. Any card can be overwritten by typing into it, so you can drop in a
moment you actually had and let the other cards do the work.

## How to work it

1. **Conversation.** Talk the hand through before writing anything down. What would you actually
   see, hear and feel in that room? Disagreement about that is the exercise.
2. **Journey.** Keep the first two cards and draw a new impact three times. Same moment, three
   places to look. Then keep the group card and re-draw the move instead.
3. **Reflection.** Ask it twice: how does this land in the room, and how does it read two weeks
   later?

## The three channels

The impact deck crosses two subjects, the group and you, with three channels:

- **Head** — cognitive. Thoughts, beliefs, the rule they infer about what is allowed here.
- **Heart** — affective. Confidence, calm and readiness give energy back; worry, fear and
  watchfulness cost more than they give.
- **Hands** — behavioural. What they do next and what they stop doing.

## Running it

One self-contained HTML file, no build step and no dependencies beyond two Google fonts. Open
`index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static host.

## One muscle at a time

Unlisted per-muscle routes narrow both behaviour decks to a single facilitation muscle and print
its definition at the top. The impact deck stays whole, because the question you trace is not
muscle-specific.

    /hold        /sense       /decide      /frame       /support     /encourage
    /discover    /challenge   /anticipate  /lead        /adapt

`/hold` gives 5 group behaviours × 5 facilitator moves × 30 impacts = 750 hands. Nothing on the
site links to these; they are for training one muscle deliberately.
